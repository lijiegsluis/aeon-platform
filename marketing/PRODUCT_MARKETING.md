# Aeon Nimbus - Product Marketing Guide

## Product Positioning

### Tagline
**"Global Financial Intelligence at Your Fingertips"**

### Elevator Pitch (30 seconds)
"Aeon Nimbus is a comprehensive financial data platform that gives investors and analysts access to detailed financial information for 319 companies across 25+ countries. Unlike Bloomberg or Capital IQ that cost thousands per month, we offer 97% financial coverage, real-time pricing, and powerful analytics starting at $29/month—or free for basic use."

### Value Proposition
- **Comprehensive:** 319 companies, 25+ countries, 97.3% financial coverage
- **Affordable:** Free tier, Professional at $29/mo vs. Bloomberg at $2,000+/mo
- **Accessible:** Web-based, mobile-responsive, no installation required
- **Powerful:** 35+ API endpoints, real-time data, advanced analytics
- **Global:** Strong emerging markets coverage (Africa, Asia, Latin America)

## Target Audiences

### Primary: Individual Investors & Analysts
**Who:** Self-directed investors, independent analysts, freelance researchers  
**Pain Points:**
- Bloomberg/FactSet too expensive ($20k+/year)
- Yahoo Finance lacks depth and reliability
- Need global data, especially emerging markets
- Want professional tools without enterprise pricing

**Message:**
> "Professional-grade financial data without the enterprise price tag. Research global companies with the same depth as institutional analysts."

### Secondary: Small Investment Firms
**Who:** Boutique funds, family offices, RIAs (5-20 people)  
**Pain Points:**
- Can't justify full Bloomberg terminals for everyone
- Need team collaboration features
- Want API access for custom workflows
- Require reliable data they can trust

**Message:**
> "Equip your entire team with institutional-quality data at a fraction of Bloomberg's cost. API access included."

### Tertiary: Academic & Research
**Who:** Finance professors, PhD students, business schools  
**Pain Points:**
- Need data for research papers
- Limited academic budgets
- Want historical data and export capabilities
- Need emerging market coverage

**Message:**
> "Comprehensive financial data for academic research. Export to CSV, analyze trends, publish with confidence."
