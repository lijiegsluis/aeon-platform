# Aeon Nimbus Platform - Demo Video Script

**Duration:** 3-4 minutes  
**Target Audience:** Financial analysts, portfolio managers, investors

---

## OPENING (0:00 - 0:20)

**Visual:** Aeon Nimbus logo → Landing page hero section  
**Voiceover:**

> "Finding reliable financial data across global markets is hard. Aeon Nimbus makes it simple. Access comprehensive financial intelligence for 319 companies across 25+ countries—all in one platform."

---

## SECTION 1: Platform Overview (0:20 - 0:50)

**Visual:** Login → Platform dashboard loads with map visualization  
**Voiceover:**

> "Log in to see your global portfolio at a glance. Our interactive map shows company locations worldwide—from Silicon Valley tech giants to African telecom leaders."

**Actions:**
- Click login (admin@aeon.local)
- Dashboard loads showing map with company pins
- Zoom into different regions (US, Europe, Africa, Asia)

---

## SECTION 2: Company Search & Data (0:50 - 1:30)

**Visual:** Search bar → Company card → Financial details  
**Voiceover:**

> "Search any company instantly. Let's look at Apple. You get real-time pricing, complete financial history, sector classification, and detailed business descriptions."

**Actions:**
- Type "Apple" in search
- Click AAPL company card
- Scroll through financial metrics:
  - Revenue: $383B
  - Net Income: $97B
  - Market Cap: Live update
  - 5-year financial history table

**Callout text overlays:**
- "97.3% financial coverage"
- "5-year historical data"
- "Real-time pricing"

---

## SECTION 3: Charts & Analytics (1:30 - 2:10)

**Visual:** Historical charts → Peer comparison  
**Voiceover:**

> "Visualize trends with historical price charts. Compare multiple stocks side-by-side. See how Apple performs against Microsoft and Google over any timeframe."

**Actions:**
- Click "View Charts" on AAPL
- Show 1Y price chart
- Click "Compare Peers"
- Select MSFT, GOOGL
- Show normalized performance chart (all starting at 100)

**Callout text overlays:**
- "Up to 10 years of data"
- "35+ API endpoints"

---

## SECTION 4: Benchmarking & Insights (2:10 - 2:40)

**Visual:** Peer benchmarking table → Sector averages  
**Voiceover:**

> "Benchmark against industry peers. See profit margins, return on equity, and how companies stack up within their sector. Spot outliers and opportunities instantly."

**Actions:**
- Navigate to "Benchmarking" tab
- Show Technology sector comparison table:
  - Profit margins: 25% avg, AAPL at 26.5%
  - ROE comparison across 10 tech companies
- Highlight best/worst performers

---

## SECTION 5: Alerts & Exports (2:40 - 3:10)

**Visual:** Watchlist → Alert setup → PDF export  
**Voiceover:**

> "Build your watchlist and set price alerts. Get notified when stocks move. Export professional PDF reports or CSV data for deeper analysis."

**Actions:**
- Click "Add to Watchlist" on AAPL
- Set 5% price alert
- Click "Export PDF"
- Show generated PDF report preview

**Callout text overlays:**
- "Email alerts"
- "Professional PDFs"
- "Mobile responsive"

---

## SECTION 6: News & Calendar (3:10 - 3:30)

**Visual:** News feed → Earnings calendar  
**Voiceover:**

> "Stay informed with integrated news feeds and earnings calendars. Never miss important dates or market-moving announcements."

**Actions:**
- Click "News" tab
- Show latest AAPL news items with thumbnails
- Switch to "Earnings Calendar"
- Show upcoming earnings dates for watchlist companies

---

## CLOSING (3:30 - 3:50)

**Visual:** Dashboard overview → Pricing page → CTA  
**Voiceover:**

> "Aeon Nimbus: Global financial intelligence at your fingertips. Start free today, upgrade when you need more power. Visit aeon-nimbus.com to get started."

**Actions:**
- Quick montage of key features
- Show pricing tiers
- End on logo + website URL

## PRODUCTION NOTES

### Required Screen Recordings:
1. Login flow (5 seconds)
2. Map interaction (10 seconds)
3. Company search and data view (20 seconds)
4. Chart comparisons (15 seconds)
5. Benchmarking table (15 seconds)
6. Watchlist and alerts (10 seconds)
7. PDF export animation (5 seconds)
8. News feed scroll (10 seconds)

### Technical Setup:
- **Resolution:** 1920x1080
- **Frame Rate:** 60fps
- **Screen Recording Tool:** OBS Studio or ScreenFlow
- **Browser:** Chrome (clean profile, no extensions visible)
- **Audio:** Professional voiceover or high-quality text-to-speech

### Visual Polish:
- Add smooth cursor animations
- Include zoom callouts for key numbers
- Use text overlays for statistics (97.3%, 319 companies, etc.)
- Background music: Subtle, corporate, non-distracting
- Color grade for consistency

### Captions:
- Include auto-generated captions for accessibility
- Highlight key terms as they're spoken

### Call-to-Action Overlay (last 10 seconds):
```
🌐 aeon-nimbus.com
✉️ demo@aeon-nimbus.com
📅 Schedule a live demo
```
