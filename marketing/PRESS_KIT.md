- **Free tools** (Yahoo Finance) have inconsistent, unreliable data

## FAQ for Media

### What makes Aeon Nimbus different from Yahoo Finance?
Yahoo Finance has inconsistent coverage and reliability issues. We provide 97.3% financial coverage with verified, enriched data from multiple sources. Our platform is designed for serious analysis, not casual browsing.

### How is it cheaper than Bloomberg?
Bloomberg serves large institutions with thousands of employees and needs 30,000+ companies. We focus on quality over quantity with 319 carefully curated companies, automated data processes, and cloud infrastructure. This lets us offer professional tools at consumer prices.

### What's your emerging markets strategy?
We believe emerging markets are underserved by US-centric platforms. We prioritize coverage of African, Asian, and Latin American companies that drive economic growth in their regions but lack visibility on global platforms.

### Who are your typical customers?
Individual investors managing their own portfolios, independent financial analysts, boutique investment firms (5-20 people), and academic researchers. Anyone who needs professional data without the enterprise price tag.

### What's next for Aeon Nimbus?
Short-term: Expand to 500+ companies by Q2 2027, add options data, improve mobile apps.  
Long-term: Cover 2,000+ companies globally, add AI-powered insights, build collaborative features for teams.
