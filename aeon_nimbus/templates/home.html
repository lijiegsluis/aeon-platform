What this platform does

Statements linked to source

Every revenue, EBITDA and net income figure links to the page of the filing it was read from. No unverifiable numbers in the model.

Models that balance

Three-statement models close by construction. If the cash flow does not reconcile, the build fails — it does not produce a wrong number.

Sector-appropriate valuation

DCF for industrials and tech, P/BV and Gordon Growth for banks, EV/EBITDA cross-checks throughout. The methodology is stated, not assumed.

Timestamped calls

Entry price, target, stop loss and position size recorded at the time of publication. Calls do not get revised after the fact.

Aeon Score™

A 0–100 composite across valuation, FCF quality, financial health and revenue momentum. One number to sort the universe.

Access by invitation

The platform is not public. Every account is issued by an administrator. Credentials are never emailed — they are passed directly.
